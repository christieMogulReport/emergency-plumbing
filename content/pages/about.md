## What this is

Plumbing Emergency Playbook is an independent information site about emergency and same-day plumbing in the United States. It collects public safety guidance and published cost data into short, practical guides.

## Safety first

<div class="callout"><strong>Gas leak?</strong> If you smell gas (often described as rotten eggs or sulfur), hear hissing near a gas line or appliance, or suspect a leak: leave the building right away, don't flip switches or use a phone inside, and call 911 from a safe distance outside. Then call your gas utility's emergency number. Do not try to find or fix the leak yourself.</div>

- **Active flooding near electricity:** don't touch electrical equipment if it is wet or if you are standing in water. If you would have to stand in water to reach the breaker panel, call an electrician or your utility.
- **Sewage backups** can contain germs. Keep children and pets away and call a plumber.
- **Anyone hurt or in danger:** call 911.

## Disclaimer

- We are **not a plumbing company, a licensed plumber, an electrician, or an insurance adviser.** We don't dispatch plumbers and we don't endorse any specific business.
- Guides are general information, not professional, legal, or insurance advice. Rules for who may shut off a valve, what work needs a licensed plumber or a permit, and what your insurance covers vary by city, state, and policy. Check your own policy and local rules.
- Costs are published national estimates from the sources linked on each page. Real quotes vary by region, time of day, and the job. Always get the price on the phone and in writing before work starts if you safely can.

## How we source

We use government agencies, insurers, and consumer cost guides, and link them. We don't publish phone numbers, prices, or reviews we haven't read at the source, and we label estimates as estimates.

## Affiliate and referral links

No affiliate links are published right now. If we add any, we will disclose it on every page that has one, and a link will never change what we recommend you do in an emergency.
