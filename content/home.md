When water is coming through the ceiling, the first ten minutes matter more than the next ten hours. These guides cover the plumbing emergencies people actually search for in the middle of the night: **burst pipes**, **sewer backups and clogs**, **leaks**, and **water heater failures**.

Every guide gives the immediate safety steps first, then when to call an emergency plumber, what to ask on the phone, and what after-hours service typically costs. Numbers are cited to their sources, and we never invent businesses, phone numbers, or prices.

**Smell gas? Leave the building, then call 911 from outside.** Don't use switches or your phone inside. See the [about and disclaimer page](/about/).

*General information only. This site is not a plumber, and nothing here replaces advice from a licensed professional.*
